Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

B-Magnet International is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle user information when you authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0 and utilize our institutional MT5 Algorithmic Systems.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in to B-Magnet using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0), we only request basic profile information permitted by you:

  • Google Identity & Email: Your verified email address, full name, and avatar picture used strictly for account creation and session authorization.
  • Trading Profile: User preferences, terminal connection states, and MT5 terminal bridge IDs.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your access to the B-Magnet MT5 Terminal and Bot Marketplace.
  • Managing your automated Expert Advisor (EA v1.21) licenses and broker bridge connections.
  • Providing live performance telemetry and account notifications.

3. Data Protection & Non-Custodial Security

B-Magnet operates under a strict Non-Custodial Architecture:

  • We do not store or manage your broker trading capital or funds. Your capital remains safely in your own regulated MT5 broker account.
  • We do not sell, rent, or distribute your personal or authentication data to third parties.
  • All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS protocols.

4. Google API User Data Policy Compliance

B-Magnet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
