Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is B-Magnet POS certified for ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing in Saudi Arabia? expand_more
Yes. B-Magnet POS is 100% compliant with ZATCA Phase 1 and Phase 2 (Fatoora) requirements. It generates compliant TLV Base64 QR codes, UUIDs, cryptographic invoice hashes, and integrates directly with ZATCA's portal for B2B clearance and B2C reporting.
What payment cards are accepted in Saudi Arabia? expand_more
B-Magnet POS accepts all local mada (مدى) debit cards, Apple Pay, Visa, Mastercard, and Web3 crypto settlement on BNB Smart Chain.
How fast are funds deposited into my Saudi bank account? expand_more
Card payments settle next business day directly into any registered Saudi bank account (Al Rajhi, SNB, Riyad Bank, etc.). Web3 crypto payments settle instantly within 2.4 seconds with zero hold periods.
How can I order the physical POS terminal hardware? expand_more
You can order the B-Magnet Terminal (599 SAR) or B-Magnet Register (1,899 SAR) directly by clicking the order button or contacting our team on WhatsApp at +91 94950 97786 or emailing bmagnet.int@gmail.com. We deliver across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, and all GCC regions within 2-3 business days.
