Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about B-Magnet POS and non-custodial crypto processing.

How do payments settle directly into my bank or crypto wallet? expand_more
B-Magnet POS is 100% non-custodial. When a customer scans or taps to pay, funds are broadcast on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20) and settle directly into your designated private Web3 wallet address within 2.4 seconds. You can convert to fiat anytime.
Are there chargeback risks with B-Magnet POS? expand_more
Zero chargeback risk. Unlike credit card networks where customers can reverse transactions months later, blockchain transactions on BNB Smart Chain are cryptographically final and irreversible.
How does the Proof-of-Stake (PoS) merchant yield work? expand_more
Merchant balances held in the B-Magnet POS network participate in decentralized network validation pools, generating daily staking distributions (estimated 14.8% APY) with zero capital lockup.
Do I need to buy physical POS hardware to start? expand_more
No. Our Starter Web3 POS works immediately on any smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer with zero hardware purchase. If you require physical thermal receipt printing and NFC hardware, our Retail Smart Terminal is available for $149.
